AI-Powered Food Recognition Using CNN

This MSc Business Analytics thesis focused on solving a real operational problem in the restaurant industry: limited visibility into food inventory leading to inefficiencies and avoidable food waste. The objective was to design a data-driven decision support solution that could automatically identify food items, track freshness over time, and provide timely insights to support better operational decisions.

I designed an end-to-end analytical solution using Convolutional Neural Networks (ResNet50 and InceptionV3) with TensorFlow, balancing model accuracy, performance, and real-world system constraints. The solution leveraged automated image recognition to update inventory records and highlight items approaching expiry, reducing reliance on manual checks.

From a business perspective, this project demonstrates how analytics and automation can improve operational efficiency, support sustainability goals, and enable earlier decision-making. It reflects my ability to frame complex technical work around clear business outcomes, data quality, and decision support.

Project: Decision Support System for Fraud Detection

This project involved the design of a Decision Support System (DSS) aimed at helping financial institutions detect potentially fraudulent transactions more effectively while operating within regulatory and compliance constraints. The focus was on enabling faster, more informed decision-making through structured data analysis and rule-based controls.

I contributed to requirements gathering, stakeholder analysis, and process design, translating business and compliance needs into functional system requirements. The solution combined a rule-based decision engine with analytical logic to flag high-risk transactions for review, supporting operational teams in prioritising investigations.

The project demonstrates my experience in bridging business, risk, and technology perspectives—ensuring solutions are not only technically sound but also aligned with governance, scalability, and regulatory expectations. It highlights my strengths in structured analysis, stakeholder collaboration, and decision-focused system design.
